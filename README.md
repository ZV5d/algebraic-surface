# Geometry of Algebraic Surfaces

**Thesis Edition v1.1 · 28 October 2026**

**Author:** Ziwei Zhang

Interactive mathematical visualizations accompanying a thesis, intended as an exploratory complement to rigorous written proofs.

## Contents

1. **Degree of a surface (Warm-up)** — Study a surface, its plane sections, and intersections with a line using adjustable 3D geometry and 2D graphs. This is the landing page.
2. **Clebsch cubic** — Projection from a point on a smooth cubic surface, blow-up geometry, residual intersections, the ramification curve, and the branch quartic.
3. **27 lines & 28 bitangents** — Interactive exploration of the 27 lines on a smooth cubic surface and the 28 bitangents of the associated plane quartic.
4. **Special configurations** — Projection from points lying on one, two, or three cubic lines, and from a point outside the cubic surface.

The experimental Trott quartic and a separate line-contraction experiment are intentionally **not** part of this thesis edition.
